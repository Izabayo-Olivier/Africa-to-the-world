HISTORY

Historical Timeline

Explore important periods and events in African and global history.

Ancient Period

African Kingdoms & Civilizations

Ancient Egypt, Nubia, Axum, Ghana, Mali and other African societies developed complex political and economic systems.

Medieval Period

Trade & Cultural Exchange

African societies participated in extensive trade networks connecting Africa with Europe, Arabia and Asia.

15th–19th Century

Atlantic Slave Trade

Millions of Africans were forcibly transported across the Atlantic through the transatlantic slave trade.

19th–20th Century

Colonialism

European powers expanded political control across much of Africa.

20th Century

Independence Movements

African countries organized political movements that eventually led to independence.
